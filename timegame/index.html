How to Play

  1. Five trains a day. The board shows the trip and how far it is.
  2. Set the lever so your train arrives exactly on time, then tap All Aboard.
  3. Miles ÷ speed = hours on the rails.
  4. Later trains add a twist. Watch for the yellow alert.
  5. Dead on time is 3 stars. Within 15 minutes is 2. Within an hour is 1.
  6. Each train gets 2 tries. Your best try counts.
