How to Play

  • Five trains. The board shows the trip and how far it is.
  • Set the lever so your train arrives exactly on time, then tap All Aboard.
  • Miles ÷ speed = hours on the rails. Later trains add a twist, so read the ticket.
  • Dead on time is 3 stars. Within 15 minutes is 2. Within an hour is 1.
  • Each train gets 2 tries. Your best try counts.
